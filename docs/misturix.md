# Misturix

The Misturix pages present the game as a tribute to Brazilian culture, familiar
food and the feeling of missing home. They use the paper, green, red and original
Feira typeface from the game's HTML documentation.

The pages are generated by `src/pages/misturix/[...slug].astro`. Translations,
contact details and privacy text live in `src/misturix/content.ts`; presentation
styles live in `src/styles/misturix.css`. They do not change the blog's shared
layout or load its scripts.

| Language | Landing page | Privacy |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Brazilian Portuguese | `/misturix/` | `/misturix/privacidade/` |
| English | `/misturix/en/` | `/misturix/en/privacy/` |
| Spanish | `/misturix/es/` | `/misturix/es/privacidad/` |
| Italian | `/misturix/it/` | `/misturix/it/privacy/` |

The language switch preserves the page type. Every page has a canonical URL,
four language alternatives and a Brazilian Portuguese default. Contact uses
`contact@woliveiras.com`, supplied by William for support and privacy questions.

## Media provenance

All media is served locally under `public/misturix/`:

- `media/wordmark.svg`, `media/selo-de-feira.svg` and the three recipe SVGs are
  copies of the game's existing branding and recipe illustrations. Recipe
  originals are `feijoada.svg`, `pudim_de_leite.svg` and `bolinho_de_chuva.svg`.
- `fonts/MisturixFeira-Regular.woff2` is the original game font. Its accompanying
  MIT license is retained in `fonts/LICENSE.txt`.
- `media/icon-512.png` and `media/feature-graphic-1024x500.png` come from the
  game's `release/google-play/pt-BR/` package. The feature image provides the
  social preview. Its generation provenance is kept in that game repository.
- `media/01-partida.png` through `media/04-resultados.png` are the unpadded,
  1080 × 2424 captures from `release/google-play/source/screenshots/`. They show
  a real Android game running in the Pixel 10 emulator configuration, with a
  demonstration profile. `02-mapa.png` shows the campaign notebook, not a
  geographic map; `03-receitas.png` shows a recipe completion.

The storefront status says the game is coming to Google Play. Add a real store
link and update that copy in all languages only when the listing is available.
Translations on this site do not claim that the game itself supports all four
languages. Gallery captions identify the Portuguese version that was captured.

## Privacy scope

The policy reflects the Android game inspected on September 17, 2026: local
progress and preferences, optional nickname, local recovery copies, voluntary
sharing through the Android chooser, and no game-operated account, analytics,
advertising or cloud synchronization. It distinguishes the static website's
hosting from the local game data and links to the providers' policies.

Review all four policy versions when the game gains a network service, SDK,
permission, account or different retention behavior. The published Android
bundle remains the source of truth. Keep the policy date in sync with revisions.
These pages contain no analytics scripts, external font requests, forms or
tracking cookies.

Run `pnpm build` to type-check, build all routes and generate the Pagefind index.
For visual review, use `pnpm dev` and open `/misturix/` at desktop and phone
widths, then switch languages on both the landing and privacy pages.
